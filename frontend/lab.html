what does prune do?
  • Deletes every article older than 60 days from articles + its cached body in reader_results.
  • Caps the reader_results table at 6,000 most-recently-used rows — older bodies fall off.
  • Runs VACUUM on the SQLite file so the freed pages actually return to the filesystem (otherwise the file just keeps growing).
  • Runs automatically once every 24 hours. Manual button below if you want to reclaim space right now.
